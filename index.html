Can I use Northstar for free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes the essentials for small teams: up to three projects, task tracking and five team members.

Can I invite my team?

Absolutely. Invite teammates by email, assign work and keep conversations connected to the projects they belong to.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Paid plans are month-to-month and can be changed or cancelled at any time from workspace settings.

Does Northstar work for remote teams?

It does. Shared priorities, comments, ownership and timelines make it easy to stay aligned across locations and time zones.
